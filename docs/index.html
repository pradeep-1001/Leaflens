The Viticulture Challenge

Why Standard Plant Apps Fail in Indian Vineyards

In the Nashik grape belt, early symptoms of Downy Mildew, Powdery Mildew, and Bacterial Canker look identical under unpredictable sunlight and harsh shadows.

🚨

Devastating Crop Losses

Downy Mildew (Kevda) can destroy up to 80% of a vineyard's export yield within 5 days of warm, humid rainfall. Preemptive indiscriminate spraying increases pesticide residue rejection rates.

☀️

Outdoor Lighting Noise

Direct sunlight causes specular glares on waxy grape leaf cuticles, causing standard smartphone AI models to confuse healthy light reflections with fungal powdery patches.

💡

The LeafLens Solution

A controlled-illumination scanning chamber (CRI 95+ LED at 45°) + Sensirion environmental sensors. Visual diagnosis is dynamically verified against microclimate infection biology.

Hardware Engineering

The Scanning Chamber Innovation

Designed by Rachi Gedam, the LeafLens chamber normalizes vineyard lighting variations into constant, lab-grade spectral conditions directly at the farm boundary.

1

Glare-Free 45° Diffused LED Ring

CRI 95+ 5500K daylight LEDs illuminate leaves at an oblique angle to eliminate specular glare on waxy foliage.

2

Sensirion SHT31 Microclimate Array

High-accuracy digital temperature and relative humidity telemetry polled via the hardware I2C bus.

3

One-Touch Autonomous Edge Trigger

Single physical button triggers Raspberry Pi camera capture, sensor packaging, and instant REST transmission.

Chamber Technical Specifications

Dimensions 20 x 20 x 25 cm (Matte Black Acrylic)
Optical Sensor Pi Camera Module 3 (Sony IMX708 Autofocus)
Illumination CRI 95+ 5500K Diffused Ring @ 45°
Environmental Telemetry SHT31 (Temp/RH) + BH1750 (Lux)
Microcontroller Raspberry Pi 4B / Zero 2 W
Interface Protocols I2C (0x44, 0x23) • HTTP REST JSON
